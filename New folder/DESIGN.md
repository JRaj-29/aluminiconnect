## Brand & Style

The design system projects academic prestige combined with modern, high-velocity SaaS efficiency. It bridges the gap between university legacy and contemporary tech platforms, fostering an atmosphere of aspiration, trust, and professional momentum for students and alumni.

The visual direction follows a modern corporate aesthetic with minimal clutter, high typographical clarity, deliberate white space, and refined functional accents. It rejects playful consumer quirks in favor of authoritative precision, crisp geometry, and confident visual hierarchy that feels native to top-tier university career ecosystems and contemporary enterprise networks.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an authoritative blue-indigo spectrum set over a crisp slate-tinted canvas. 

- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Drives primary actions, active navigational states, and focus triggers.
- **Secondary (`#4F46E5`)**: Used for key mentorship relationship milestones, featured profile highlights, and contextual interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#0EA5E9`)**: Reserved for informational badges, metrics, and secondary analytical points.
- **Neutrals (`#0F172A` to `#F8FAFC`)**: A cold-slate neutral scale providing high-contrast text legibility, crisp architectural dividers, and layered background surfaces.
- **Semantic Accents**: Strict functional cues dictate mentorship status:
  - Emerald (`#059669` / `#ECFDF5` bg): Active mentorship, accepted requests, verified alumni status.
  - Amber (`#D97706` / `#FFFBEB` bg): Pending invitations, action-required schedules, waiting reviews.
  - Rose (`#E11D48` / `#FFF1F2` bg): Declined matches, expired requests, destructive actions.

## Typography

The type scale combines Plus Jakarta Sans for structural personality with Inter for systematic, high-density clarity.

Headlines employ tight tracking and semi-bold/bold weights to anchor dashboard views, mentor profiles, and directory headers. Body text uses Inter's optimized metrics to maintain high readability across dense alumni resumes, mentorship conversation threads, and onboarding pathways. Labels, statuses, and data chips rely on calibrated uppercase or medium/semi-bold variants with micro letter-spacing to ensure instant scannability.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on a 12-column responsive fluid grid capped at a maximum layout width of 1280px to guarantee balanced proportions on wide desktop monitors.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (1.5rem) gutters, 32px (2rem) page margins. Mentorship discovery layouts leverage 3-column or 4-column card matrices with fixed horizontal filters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, 16px (1rem) gutters, 24px (1.5rem) margins. Directory filters collapse into an off-canvas drawer; mentor cards reflow into 2-column configurations.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (1rem) gutters, 16px (1rem) margins. Cards stack to single-column full-width layouts; secondary profile metadata shifts below identity headers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid approach: low-contrast slate outlines paired with ambient, tinted drop shadows. Heavy blurs and aggressive drop shadows are avoided to maintain an architectural, editorial discipline.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Background rendered in slate tint (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Level 1 (Default Containers & Cards)**: Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) surface framed by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), supported by an ambient drop shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns)**: Elevated white surface with a tightened border (`#CBD5E1`) and a soft diffused shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Mentorship Scheduling Drawers)**: High surface elevation, `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, bounded by a subtle 1px border.

## Shapes

The design system operates with calibrated `rounded-xl` logic for primary structural elements, balanced by tighter radii for embedded controls:

- Structural cards, modals, and panel wrappers utilize a 1rem (16px) corner radius, providing an approachable modern shell without appearing overly geometric or juvenile.
- Interactive controls (buttons, inputs, select fields) adhere strictly to a 0.5rem (8px) radius for clean, compact execution.
- Status badges and tag pills utilize fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to visually differentiate metadata from clickable interactive cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#2563EB` fill, white text, 0.5rem radius, medium weight. Subtle active scale down (`0.98`) with hover shift to `#1D4ED8`.
- **Secondary**: Surface white with 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text, hover background `#F8FAFC` and border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background, `#475569` text, `#F1F5F9` on hover.

### Status Pills & Chips
- Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) with 1px matched borders and 10% opacity tints.
  - **Active / Accepted**: Background `#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`.
  - **Pending**: Background `#FFFBEB`, border `#FDE68A`, text `#92400E`.
  - **Rejected / Closed**: Background `#FFF1F2`, border `#FECDD3`, text `#9F1239`.
- **Topic Tags (Skills, Industries)**: Background `#F1F5F9`, border `#E2E8F0`, text `#334155`, font size `0.75rem`.

### Input Fields
- White fill, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 0.5rem radius, 0.875rem typography.
- Focus state activates a 1px `#2563EB` boundary accompanied by a 3px soft ring `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 1rem × 1rem. Border `#94A3B8` in idle state; active filled with `#2563EB` displaying an SVG white check or inner dot.

### Cards (Alumni & Mentorship Matches)
- White container with `rounded-xl` (1rem) styling, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, ambient level-1 shadow.
- Structured into clear segments: Top header (Avatar, Name, Grad Year, University Emblem/Major), Middle body (Current Role, Company, Bio snippet, Industry tags), Bottom footer (Action button: "Request Mentorship", Availability slot indicators).

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Mentorship Milestone Tracker**: A horizontal stepped line with check states in emerald and upcoming requirements in slate.
- **Office Hours Availability Slot**: A segmented grid button showing date, time, and session mode (Video, Portfolio Review, Coffee Chat) with an active outline indicator.
- **Alumni Verification Badge**: An inline pill consisting of a checkmark shield icon, `#4F46E5` color, and text designating verified graduation year.
